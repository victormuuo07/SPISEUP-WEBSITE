Our mission

Better tasting everyday food

SpiseUp exists to put a bolder, cleaner seasoning on every table in Kenya — starting right here in Kitengela.

What we believe

Table seasoning in Kenya has long meant plain salt or imported blends full of additives. SpiseUp was built to change that — a spicy mix made from real African bird's eye chili, cayenne, paprika, garlic and onion, with nothing artificial hiding in the ingredients list.

We call it "the SpiseUp effect": guests sprinkle it once, notice the difference immediately, and start asking hotels and restaurants where they can buy their own bottle.

What makes us different

  • 🌶 Real chili, not fillers — African bird's eye chili is the heart of every batch.
  • 🚫 No MSG, ever — 100% natural ingredients, clearly labelled.
  • 🤝 Built with our retailers — pricing and pack sizes shaped by feedback from the hotels who stock us.
  • 📦 Reliable supply — SMS-based restocking keeps our regular accounts from ever running out.
