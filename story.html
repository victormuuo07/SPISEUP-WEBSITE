How we got here

From free samples to 100+ hotel accounts

SpiseUp grew the way most good things in Kitengela do — one hotel table, one taste, one referral at a time.

Idea

Finding the right product

Our founders set out to build a Kenyan food brand with a fast, clear path to market — and a spicy, all-natural table seasoning fit the bill.

Launch

Free sampling on the ground

We started by handing out samples directly to hotels and nyama choma spots around Kitengela, letting the product speak for itself on the table.

Growth

50 hotels to 100+

Word of mouth from guests turned early trials into repeat orders. Our hotel and restaurant accounts grew from around 20 to more than 100, with restocking now supported by SMS alerts.

Today

Beyond the hotel table

We're expanding into schools, mini supermarkets and mama mboga stalls, while working through full Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS) certification to support national growth.

What guides us

🌶

Real ingredients

African bird's eye chili, cayenne, paprika, garlic and onion — nothing artificial.

🤝

Local first

Built with Kitengela's hotels, schools and stalls, not for a distant market.

📦

Never out of stock

Restocking is built into how we work, so our partners never lose a sale.
